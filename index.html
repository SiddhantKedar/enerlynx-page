What We Do

Built for the Demands of Solar Generation

Enerlynx designs industrial IoT hardware and software for solar power plants across India. Our ELX-GW100 gateway sits at the heart of every installation — collecting data from inverters, meters, and protection devices, then securely relaying it to cloud platforms and regulatory reporting infrastructure.

From small rooftop systems to utility-scale plants, our edge gateway delivers the visibility, reliability, and compliance connectivity that modern solar operations demand.

System Design

Dual-Node Topology

Every solar plant installation uses two ELX-GW100 nodes — one at the generation site capturing inverter and meter data, and one at the Grid Sub-Station monitoring grid interconnection. Both nodes publish independently to the cloud.

This dual-node approach provides complete end-to-end visibility and satisfies MNRE and SLDC regulatory reporting requirements from a single unified platform.
